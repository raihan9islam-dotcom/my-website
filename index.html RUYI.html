Our Story

A modern room, built around the grill.

RUYI — 如意 — means "as you wish." It's the idea behind the room: a charcoal fire at the centre of the table, dishes shared slowly, and a menu that leaves space for what you're in the mood for tonight.

Every skewer is grilled to order over live charcoal. Every sauce is made in-house. The rest — the warm light, the quiet music, the long tables — is built for people who like to stay a while.
